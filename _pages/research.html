RESEARCH

Connecting the past
with the present.

I study how climate variability, geological processes, and human activity shape aquatic ecosystems—and how those changes are recorded in sediments. My work spans decades to millennia, from Patagonia to North American lakes and coastal systems.

01 / PALEOLIMNOLOGY & PALEOENVIRONMENTS

Reading environmental history.

I reconstruct hydroclimatic and ecological change using sediment cores, stratigraphy, diatom and phytolith assemblages, and geochemical indicators. Interpreting these archives requires understanding both the changes in the record and the processes that created it.

Questions & projects

  • Late Holocene hydroclimate in arid and semi-arid Argentine lakes
  • Coastal environmental change and sea-level history in a Florida salt marsh
  • Modern ecological context for interpreting sedimentary proxies

02 / GEOMORPHOLOGY & SEDIMENT DYNAMICS

How landscapes shape the record.

I combine bathymetric mapping, satellite imagery, field observations, and sedimentology to investigate basin evolution, hydrologic connectivity, sediment resuspension, and carbon storage. These processes help explain why nearby lakes can respond differently to the same climatic forcing.

Questions & projects

  • Drought, basin morphometry, and the distribution of lake habitats
  • Natural and human influences on Patagonian watersheds
  • Tidal-flat dynamics and sedimentary processes linked to carbon storage

03 / MODERN LIMNOLOGY & ENVIRONMENTAL DATA

Observing processes as they happen.

I use field monitoring, experiments, and reproducible data workflows to investigate lake clarity, nutrients, primary producers, particles, and invasive species. Linking these observations to sedimentary records strengthens interpretations of long-term ecosystem change.

Questions & projects

  • Leading TEON platform development: integration, QA/QC, and access
  • Particle dynamics and Lake Tahoe clarity
  • Microplastics in freshwater ecosystems
  • Golden mussel survival and reproductive viability in lakes Tahoe, Mead, and Pyramid
  • Wildfire effects and habitat-specific lake metabolism

Tahoe Environmental Observatory Network

I lead the development of the TEON platform, bringing environmental observations together through data integration, quality assurance and quality control (QA/QC), and public access.

This work connects field monitoring with the data infrastructure needed to explore change across Lake Tahoe’s watershed and aquatic environments.
